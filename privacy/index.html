Privacy policy

What this website collects, which is deliberately close to nothing, and what happens when you email us.

Draft — pending legal review. This document is a structural template prepared alongside the website. It has not been reviewed by counsel, and the company's registration details, jurisdiction and data-processing specifics have not been finalised. Do not rely on it as legal advice or as a concluded policy.

This website

  • This site is served as static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no advertising and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or pixels.
  • No personal data is collected, profiled or sold by visiting these pages. Server-side access logs, if kept by the host, are the hosting provider's records rather than ours.

When you email us

  • Messages you send to the addresses on the contact page are used to answer you and to keep a record of the request. They are not added to a marketing list.
  • Do not send credentials, private keys or personal data about other people. Security reports should describe the issue without including live secrets.

Products

  • Products in the GisCode family are local-first: by default, inference, files and test execution stay on your machine. An optional hosted writer sends model requests to a remote endpoint only if you enable it and supply your own key.
  • Products that store data locally keep it in files you control. This policy does not describe a hosted service, because none is offered yet.

Advertising (planned, not active)

  • No advertising runs on this site today: no ad script is loaded, no ad cookie is set and no advertising identifier is used.
  • If Google AdSense is enabled after review, Google and its partners may set cookies and use device identifiers to serve and measure ads. That requires this policy to be rewritten and a consent mechanism added where law requires one, before the first ad request.
  • Advertising would never appear inside a download flow, and no ad would be labelled as part of the software.

Your choices

  • You can ask what correspondence we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted, by writing to the privacy address listed on the contact page.

Last structural revision: November 2026. Contact details are on the contact page.
