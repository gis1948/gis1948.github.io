Safety and responsible use

العربية

GisWind is a player. What it plays is chosen by you, and your provider account is the thing worth protecting.

GisWind ships no content

No channels, playlists, subscriptions or credentials are included. There is no bundled content and no provider account. You add the sources you are entitled to use.

Protect your provider account

  • Never share your provider username or password with anyone — including anyone claiming to be support. GisWind staff never ask for them.
  • Do not post playlist URLs in forums, screenshots or chat groups: a playlist URL often contains your account.
  • Use a password you do not reuse elsewhere.
  • If you suspect your line was shared or resold, change the password with your provider.

Install only from us

Download GisWind from our downloads page or from a copy you made yourself. Builds posted on file hosts, forums or messaging apps may be modified. Compare the SHA-256 published with each release before installing or updating.

Choose lawful sources

Use providers you pay for, or content you are licensed to access. GisWind cannot tell whether a source is lawful, and using it does not make an unlawful source lawful. Responsibility for the sources you add rests with you.

Report a bad source or a bad build

If a source stops working, looks pirated, or a download did not come from us, tell us through the contact page. Include the playlist name (not the full URL), what happened, and the version shown in About GisWind. Please do not redistribute the link.

If something goes wrong

  • Settings → Save diagnostics writes a redacted report to your Desktop; send it with your message.
  • Settings → Delete my account removes that account and its playlists from the PC; exported backups are unaffected.
  • Settings → Open log folder shows the local logs.
