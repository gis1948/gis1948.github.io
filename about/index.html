About Jusoor

A software company that would rather be accurate than impressive.

Jusoor builds AI systems, developer tools and engineering software. The family you see on this site spans a local-first coding agent, an AI companion, design and media tooling, and a community platform — developed in the open where that is safe, and privately where it is not.

Mission

Build software that people can depend on for real work: tools that run on hardware you control, that keep your data where you put it, and that tell you the truth about what they did.

That last part is the hard one. AI systems are unusually good at sounding finished. So we treat verification — executed tests, byte-level read-backs, dated evidence files — as the product, not the paperwork after it.

Engineering vision

Everything we build shares three assumptions. Compute is local and personal; a model is a component, not an oracle; and a refusal with a clear reason is more useful than a guess.

The GisCode agent is where this is most visible: a bounded set of deterministic operations that work with no model at all, and a workflow that stops and reports rather than looping on a failure it cannot fix.

Identity

JusoorAI is the company and this website. Jusoor is the brand the products carry, and the name of the community platform. Gis is the mark itself: two squares in rotation, sixteen points, one geometry recoloured for every product.
