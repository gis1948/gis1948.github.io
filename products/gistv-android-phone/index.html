GisTvm for Android

The unrestricted phone client.

The Android phone edition: the full IPTV experience plus profiles, an in-app smart-link banner and the account model shared with the desktop app.

Ask about GisTvm for Android

Screenshots pending. Phone screenshots of the unrestricted client, plus a short clip of profile switching.

One of four clients in the GisTV family.

What it does

  • The same channel navigation, favourites and player model as the TV client, sized for touch and one-handed use.
  • Profile handling with a guest mode that keeps data on the device.
  • An in-app WebView surface for the smart-link banner, with a graceful "content unavailable" fallback rather than a blank frame.

Sync, precisely

  • A SyncManager interface exists and the call sites are wired, but the shipping implementation is a local no-op: cloud sync is not active in this build.
  • The cross-device sync protocol and its server are designed and specified, not shipped.

What is not there yet

  • No release APK is published; only a debug build exists in the tree and debug packages are not distributable.
  • Cloud sync is designed but disabled; do not expect favourites to follow you between devices yet.
  • No phone screenshots have been captured for publication.
