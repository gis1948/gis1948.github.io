Jusoor

A community and business connection platform for Kansas City's Arab community.

Members, businesses and the connections between them: profiles, market and services, events, KC Kitchen, Deals and News, with a direct line from customer to business.

Ask about Jusoor

Screenshots pending. Screenshots of the seven-section mobile app, an official app icon and store artwork, and a redacted capture of the admin console. None of these exist in the project yet.

Community and members

  • Member profiles: who a person is in the community, not just an account row.
  • Seven-section navigation — Home, Events, Community, Quiz, Games, Account and Settings — with an in-app English/Arabic toggle and a full right-to-left layout.
  • Home opens on an Adhan card built from real prayer times, with Hijri date conversion and community announcements.

Businesses and connections

  • Business profiles sitting beside member profiles, so a shop, restaurant or service is found where the people already are.
  • Connections between members and businesses — the graph between the two is the product, not a directory dump.
  • Customer-to-business messaging, so a question reaches the business as a conversation rather than a phone number hunt.

Market, services and what is on

  • Market and services listings for buying, selling and offering work inside the community.
  • Events, KC Kitchen, Deals and News: what is happening, what is cooking, what is discounted and what changed.
  • Jobs, mosque and medical-clinic directories, plus a quiz section, all reachable from the same navigation.

How it is being built

  • The iOS client is a buildable SwiftUI app: theme, navigation, auth flow and core screens are implemented and run against mock data so the project compiles immediately.
  • A desktop admin console manages platform content as an internal operations tool with an administrative service account, not a public product.
  • The Android client and the services behind accounts, messaging and listings are the outstanding work between this design and a release.

What is not there yet

  • Jusoor is not published to any app store.
  • Authentication is mocked in the iOS client and the platform services behind accounts, messaging and business profiles are not integrated yet. Everything above describes the designed product; nobody can sign in today.
  • The admin console is internal, requires private credentials and is not distributed with this website.
  • There is no official Jusoor logo, app icon or store artwork in the project yet — the mark on this page is the JusoorAI company identity, not a product logo.
