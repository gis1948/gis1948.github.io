GisPM

Project management and field inspection for engineering work.

The plan, the site and the paperwork in one place: projects and tasks, field inspections with photo evidence, reports, and the documents a job has to hand over at the end.

Ask about GisPM

Screenshots pending. No build, screenshot, logo or store artwork exists yet; there is nothing to capture.

Intended scope

  • Projects and tasks: work broken down to something a person can pick up, with an owner and a date.
  • Field inspections: checklists answered on site against a project, with photographic evidence attached to the item being inspected.
  • Reporting: an inspection result that becomes a report without being retyped into a document afterwards.
  • Documentation: drawings, certificates and photographs kept with the project instead of scattered across shared folders.
  • Productivity: the next action visible without asking someone for a status update.

Where it stands

  • GisPM has no source project, build, screenshot or release date. The list above is designed scope, written down so the product has a stated shape before it has code.
  • A product that says it is unbuilt is more useful than one that pretends to be in beta.

What is not there yet

  • Nothing is built: no public build, no installer, no documentation and no release date.
  • Field inspections, reporting and documentation are the intended design, not features anyone can use today.
  • No official GisPM logo or artwork exists; the mark on this page is the JusoorAI company identity.
