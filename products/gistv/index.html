GisTV

Live TV, movies and series, on four screens.

One IPTV family across Android TV, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, and Windows: playlists and Xtream accounts, channels, EPG, favourites and search, with playback that stays on your own network.

Inside the product

The four clients

GisTV is not one app. It is four clients on a shared player and navigation model, plus a sync service whose TV client is deliberately left out.

One family, four clients

  • GisTV for Android TV (com.gis.iptv.tv): lean-back television with categories, series, VOD and local favourites.
  • GisTvm for Android (com.gis.iptv): the unrestricted phone client with profiles and guest mode.
  • GisTvm for iOS: a native SwiftUI iPhone and iPad client with Arabic right-to-left support.
  • GisWind: the Windows desktop client, with a local media server for paired devices on the same network.

What the player does

  • Plays M3U and M3U8 playlists, whether a local file or a remote URL, and Xtream Codes accounts.
  • EPG, favourites, categories and search across the whole catalogue rather than only the list in front of you.
  • Channel management: grouping, ordering and favourite groups that survive a restart.
  • A built-in web server with WebRTC, so a browser on the same network can watch what the device is playing.
  • Phone and PC pair over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account to create and no server of ours in the middle.
  • Fullscreen playback hides the system bars and restores them on exit.

Where your data lives

  • Playlists, favourites and settings are stored locally on each device.
  • The Android build reports anonymous app-open and crash events so bugs get fixed; no personal data is uploaded or sold.
  • Provider credentials sit in the local database at rest, and cleartext HTTP remains permitted because typical IPTV sources require it.

One navigation model, four apps

  • Next/Previous cycles inside the list you started from, never across the whole catalogue — the rule that makes a remote feel predictable.
  • The clients share a player and a navigation lineage instead of being four unrelated apps that happen to look alike.

What is not there yet

  • Android TV and Windows builds exist and were hashed for this review, but the project tree carries no LICENSE or EULA, so redistribution rights are unverified and nothing is offered for download.
  • The Android phone client has only a debug build; the iOS client archives unsigned and is not on the App Store.
  • Cloud sync across devices is designed and its protocol written down, but not shipped. What exists today is pairing on the local network, not favourites following you between phones.
  • The official app icon and the Android TV store artwork are real and used on this page. No equivalent artwork exists yet for the iOS or Windows clients.
